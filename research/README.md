# Research

`coverage-test.mjs` checks 30 employers' public job boards and counts US postings that state pay.
`coverage-results-2026-11-14.txt` is its output from 14 November 2026: 25 of 27 reachable employers
published pay, covering 84% of their US postings.

Run it with `node research/coverage-test.mjs` (Node 18 or newer, no key needed).

The sample is tech and fintech companies, chosen because they hire in states with pay-transparency
laws. It is the best case for coverage, not a picture of all employers.
